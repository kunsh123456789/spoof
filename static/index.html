📱 Phones & connection
    Wi-Fi setup (no cable)
    1. Plug the iPhone in once, tap Trust, and turn on Developer Mode.
    2. Switch on Allow connecting over Wi-Fi above (needs the cable).
    3. Put the Mac and iPhone on the same Wi-Fi network and keep the iPhone awake and unlocked.
    4. Unplug. Within a few seconds the phone shows a Wi-Fi badge. If not, press Find Wi-Fi phones. On a Mac you may also need Finder → your iPhone → Show this iPhone when on Wi-Fi.

    Wi-Fi is newer than the cable and can be less reliable; iOS 17.0–17.3 over Wi-Fi may need the tunneld command from the README. Controlling two iOS 17+ phones at once also needs tunneld.

    📡 No internet connection — saved offline areas still work.

      How it works
      1. First set a starting location in the Place tab.
      2. Choose a path type: Straight line goes directly; Walking / Driving follow real roads.
      3. Choose a speed, then go to one destination (the pin), build a multi-stop route, or play a GPX file.
      4. In a multi-stop route each stop can have its own path type and speed (walk to the car, then drive).
      5. Return to start sends you back where the trip began. Save route keeps it in the Saved tab.
      6. The Realism switches below make the movement less robotic.

        How it works
        1. Draw your area: press Draw area, then click points on the map around the neighborhood you want. Click the first point again (or press Finish) to close the shape. Skip this to use 350 m around the pin instead.
        2. Press Find houses. 🎃 pumpkins appear on the map — real houses from OpenStreetMap inside your area, ordered into a house-to-house tour.
        3. Press Start. The phone jumps to the first house, then walks street to street at a natural pace and pauses 20–120 s at every door, like a real trick-or-treater.
        4. Watch progress in the bottom bar. Stop moving ends it early — the phone stays where it is.

        While this tab is open the app turns orange so you know trick-or-treat mode is active.

                How it works
                1. While you have internet, zoom the map so the neighborhood you'll use fills the screen.
                2. Give it a name and press Download what I see. The app saves the map, the houses and the streets for that area on your Mac (a few MB).
                3. Later with no data, everything works inside that area: the map still shows, Find houses uses the saved houses, and walking/driving paths follow the saved streets.
                4. Dashed boxes on the map show which areas are saved.

                For trick-or-treat, make sure your whole neighborhood fits on screen (up to about 2.4 × 2.4 km). Map zoom levels 12–17 are saved; zooming in further offline shows blank tiles.
